STAGE 02 — Ground items spawn, render, and despawn
STATUS: DONE 2026-07-08, round 3 (code-complete, compiles; pending human live-test). Round 1
(region-tracking) and Round 2 (queueZonePayload build-area-relative coordinate math) got items
rendering; Round 3 fixed a missing "Take" option (ObjAdd's opFlags param was hiding all
cache-defined right-click options). See HANDOFF.txt "STAGE 02" → "Round 3" for the full writeup.
DEPENDS ON: Stage 00. Stage 01's BASELINE-FINDINGS.txt helps but isn't required.
Follow the RULES in stages/README.txt.

## GOAL
A ground item spawned (by command, by drop once Stage 05 lands, or by NPC death once Stage 03/09
land) renders on the correct tile for a rev-228 client, and disappears when picked up or expired.

## SYMPTOM (human-reported 2026-07-08)
"Ground Items do not spawn/render/exist - something is completely broken here."

## VERIFIED FACTS (checked against code, not folklore)
1. PacketSender.java line ~92: queueZonePayload() wraps zone payloads in UpdateZonePartialEnclosed
   with `x & ~0x7, y & ~0x7` (absolute zone corner). This is a 2026-07-08 fix (the old `x >> 3`
   form silently targeted the wrong zone) that has NEVER been live-tested. It may be correct now,
   or still wrong — do not assume either way.
2. PacketSender.sendGroundItem (line ~736) has an RSProt path (ObjAdd). BUT
   sendRemoveGroundItem (line ~754) has NO RSProt path at all — it goes straight to the legacy
   sendMapPacket, which no-ops for RSProt players (null Netty channel). Ground item REMOVAL was
   never ported. RSProt rev-228 has an ObjDel zone payload class for this — port it through
   queueZonePayload exactly like ObjAdd (verify its constructor against the osrs-228-model
   sources jar in the Gradle cache, same as every other payload was).
   While there, note sendObjectAnimation (line ~791) also has no RSProt port (LocAnim) — same
   pattern, cheap to add in passing since you're in the same file with the same sources open.
3. STRONG UNVERIFIED HYPOTHESIS for "items never appear at all": zone-content (re)send timing.
   Find every call site of sendGroundItem / GroundItem's own send logic (likely
   DMS/src/main/java/io/dm/model/map/ground/GroundItem.java — check its sendAdd()/per-player
   loop) and determine WHEN items get sent to a player: (a) the moment the item spawns, and
   (b) when a player enters a region / rebuilds their map. Path (b) is suspect: legacy servers
   typically resend zone contents when the client acknowledges a region load via an incoming
   packet (see network/incoming/handlers/RegionUpdateHandler.java — a legacy-184 packet the
   rev-228 client never sends), or hook lastRegion changes. If the resend hook never fires for
   RSProt players, items spawned before you entered the area never appear, and items spawned in
   front of you only appear if path (a) fires and the zone coords are right. Check what
   RsProt.maybeRebuildRegion (RsProt.java ~line 831) does after a RebuildNormal — zone contents
   are almost certainly NOT resent after a rebuild today.

## STEPS
1. Read sendGroundItem/sendRemoveGroundItem in PacketSender.java and GroundItem.java's send
   lifecycle. Map out exactly when a ground item packet is sent to whom.
2. Port ObjDel for sendRemoveGroundItem (mirror the ObjAdd pattern; constructor field order from
   the sources jar, not guessed).
3. Root-cause the spawn-visibility failure using the facts above. Likely fixes are one or both:
   coordinate form still wrong in queueZonePayload; and/or region-load/rebuild resend never
   firing for RSProt players (fix by resending zone contents for the new build area after
   RebuildLogin/RebuildNormal, or hooking the existing resend logic into the RSProt path).
4. Compile. Hand the human the TEST SCRIPT.

## TEST SCRIPT (human)
1. Log in, spawn a ground item at your feet via the GroundItemCommands admin command. It must
   appear on YOUR EXACT TILE (not offset, not elsewhere).
2. Pick it up — it must vanish from the ground and land in inventory (pickup routes through
   GroundItemActionHandler which is confirmed wired; if inventory itself misbehaves that's
   Stage 05's problem — for THIS stage only the ground model vanishing matters).
3. Spawn another, walk two regions away (~2 map squares), walk back: item must still be visible.
4. Have the item expire (if a timer exists) or spawn+remove via command: model must disappear.

## SUCCESS CRITERIA
All four test-script points confirmed by the human on a real rev-228 client.

## NON-GOALS
Drop-from-inventory (Stage 05 owns the click that triggers it). Projectiles/graphics/area sounds
(Stage 10 verifies those — but note anything you learn about queueZonePayload correctness in
their stage file, since all six sends share that helper).
